# Brand Strategy & Identity Architecture

This skill provides comprehensive methodologies and runbooks for Brand Managers and Strategists to develop world-class brand identities, distinctive brand assets, tone-of-voice frameworks, and positioning strategies.

---

## 1. Brand Core & Strategic Positioning

### Brand DNA Pyramid
1. **Core Purpose**: Why does this entity exist beyond making money?
2. **Core Values**: 3 to 5 non-negotiable principles that drive every product decision.
3. **Brand Personality / Archetype**:
   * *The Magician* (Transformational, visionary, cinematic — e.g., Apple, Disney)
   * *The Creator* (Innovation, craftsmanship, bespoke excellence — e.g., Figma, Lego)
   * *The Hero* (Mastery, courage, triumph against odds — e.g., Nike)
   * *The Sage* (Wisdom, intelligence, truth — e.g., Google, BBC)
4. **Value Proposition Formula**:
   $$\text{For } [\text{Target Audience}], \text{ who } [\text{Problem/Need}], [\text{Brand/Product}] \text{ is the } [\text{Category}] \text{ that } [\text{Key Benefit}], \text{ unlike } [\text{Alternative}].$$

## 2. Tone of Voice & Copywriting Matrix

| Dimension | Low Extreme | Selected Profile | High Extreme | Implementation Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formality** | Slang / Casual | **Polished & Authoritative** | Academic / Stiff | Use contractions sparingly; speak with quiet confidence. |
| **Energy** | Reserved / Stoic | **Cinematic & Inspiring** | Hyperactive / Hype | Evoke grandeur and depth; use evocative, rhythmic cadence. |
| **Simplicity** | Jargon-heavy | **Direct & Lucid** | Childlike | Express complex ideas in sharp, punchy phrases. |
| **Humor** | Deadpan / Serious | **Nuanced Wit** | Slapstick / Memes | Subtle, intellectual charm; never cheap puns. |

## 3. Visual Identity & Design Token Rules

### The 60-30-10 Color Harmonization
* **60% Dominant Canvas**: Deep obsidian/charcoal or crisp minimalist backdrop (`--bg-primary`).
* **30% Structural Secondary**: Elevational panels, subtle borders, card surfaces (`--bg-surface`, `--border-subtle`).
* **10% High-Impact Accent**: Hero buttons, focal glows, key badges (`--accent-gold`, `--accent-crimson`, `--accent-cyan`).

### Typography Pairing Principles
1. **Headline Font (Display/Character)**: Strong personality (e.g., `Cinzel`, `Outfit`, `Syne`, `Playfair Display`).
2. **Body Font (Reading/Workhorse)**: Extreme legibility at 14px-18px (e.g., `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Roboto`).
3. **Monospace (Data/Code/Precision)**: Clean tabular figures (e.g., `JetBrains Mono`, `Space Mono`).

## 4. Brand Consistency Audit Checklist

- [ ] Does every heading resonate with the core brand archetype?
- [ ] Are accent colors applied to no more than 10% of visual elements on screen?
- [ ] Is contrast compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA standards for all body copy?
- [ ] Are micro-copy elements (buttons, error states, empty states) speaking in the unified brand voice?
- [ ] Is there a clear favicon, OpenGraph banner, and meta summary aligned with the identity?
