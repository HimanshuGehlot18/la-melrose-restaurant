About Executive Chef

Meet Alessandro Rossi

Born amidst the rolling hills and olive groves of Tuscany, Alessandro learned the sacred harmony of hand-rolled pasta and wood fire in his family’s century-old trattoria.

Following fifteen years directing Michelin-starred kitchens in Florence, Lyon, and Milan, Alessandro founded La Melrose to unite brasserie warmth with culinary precision. Every sauce is simmered for over 18 hours, every pasta bronze-extruded daily, and every wine personally paired with small-estate Italian vintners.
